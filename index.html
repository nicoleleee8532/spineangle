How to measure

Put the lines on the two most tilted vertebrae

Vertebrae are the stacked blocks down the middle of the X-ray. Each has a bright top edge and bottom edge. Raise the contrast if the edges are hard to see.

  1. 1. Find the bendThe flattest vertebrae sit in the middle of the curve.
  2. 2. Find the most tilted one above itMove up from the middle. Tilt gets bigger, then smaller again. Pick the one that tilts most.
  3. 3. Tap its top edgeLeft corner first, then right corner.
  4. 4. Do the same belowFind the vertebra tilted most the other way. Tap its bottom edge, left then right.

    Getting help in the UK

    Free physiotherapy on the NHS

    NHS physiotherapy is free. Ask your GP for a referral, or check whether your local NHS musculoskeletal (MSK) physiotherapy service lets you self-refer; many areas do. International students who paid the Immigration Health Surcharge are covered. University students can also ask their student health centre.

    When to get help quickly
    • New numbness, tingling or weakness in your legs
    • Changes in bladder or bowel control
    • Shortness of breath that's getting worse

    Call 111, or 999 in an emergency. See your GP soon if your back shape is visibly changing or pain is getting worse.
